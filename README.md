## Abstract

The **AI-Based Smart Food Redistribution and Waste Reduction System** aims to reduce food wastage using artificial intelligence and data-driven decision-making. The system identifies surplus food and facilitates its redistribution to people or organizations in need.

It analyzes food availability, quantity, expiry time, location, and demand to identify suitable redistribution opportunities. AI-based prediction and matching techniques prioritize food based on urgency and connect surplus food with nearby recipients or organizations.

The system provides an efficient, technology-driven approach to reducing avoidable food waste while improving food redistribution and resource utilization.

## Objectives

* Develop an AI-based system to identify and manage surplus food.
* Predict food demand and potential food wastage.
* Prioritize food based on quantity, expiry time, and urgency.
* Match surplus food with suitable nearby recipients or organizations.
* Reduce avoidable food waste through intelligent redistribution.
* Provide a centralized platform for monitoring food availability and redistribution.
* Improve the efficiency and transparency of food redistribution.

## System Workflow

1. **Food Data Collection** – Collect surplus food details such as quantity, expiry time, location, and availability.
2. **Data Processing** – Clean and preprocess the collected data.
3. **AI-Based Prediction** – Predict potential food wastage and redistribution requirements.
4. **Food Prioritization** – Prioritize food based on expiry time, quantity, and urgency.
5. **Smart Matching** – Match surplus food with suitable recipients or organizations.
6. **Redistribution** – Facilitate the redistribution of suitable surplus food.
7. **Monitoring and Results** – Track redistribution activities and measure food-waste reduction.
